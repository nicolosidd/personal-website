AI Outfit Planner & Second-Hand Marketplace

Overview

Drape suggests the perfect outfit every morning from the clothes you already own, based on weather, calendar and style. When your wardrobe has a gap, an AI-powered second-hand marketplace finds the piece that fills it.

  • Role: Product Manager — problem framing, research, AI-assisted prototyping
  • Platform: Mobile (iOS prototype)
  • Status: Prototype complete, MVP testing next

The Problem

A full closet and "nothing to wear": most people rotate a small share of what they own and lose time every morning deciding.

The usual fix is buying something new, often fast fashion that adds to the pile and to the environmental cost. The real need isn't more clothes, it's getting more out of the ones we have — and buying smarter when something is truly missing.

The Idea

"Dress for the life you live."

Drape knows your wardrobe, your weather and your day. Every morning it proposes one complete look, explains why it fits, and lets you confirm it with a tap.

When the AI notices a missing piece that would unlock more outfits, it doesn't push something new. It finds a second-hand option that works with what you already own.

How It Works

  • Digitize — Snap a photo of each piece and add products informations helped by AI.
  • Plan — Each morning, the AI combines weather, calendar and style into one look.
  • Wear — Confirm with "I'll wear this" and get a reminder ready for the next day.
  • Complete — The marketplace suggests second-hand pieces that fill your wardrobe's gaps.

The Marketplace: Circular by Design

The marketplace doesn't sell more clothes; it sells the right missing piece. The AI reads your wardrobe, spots its gaps and pulls live listings from resale platforms such as Vestiaire Collective and Vinted.

Each card explains why it's worth buying:

  • Fit with your wardrobe: "Completes 8 looks" or "Works with your denim";
  • Impact: CO₂ saved or reduced impact compared to buying new;
  • Value: condition, second-hand price and original price side by side.

Filters like For you, Layers and Accessories keep browsing short and focused.

Process: From Research to Working Prototype with AI

I skipped static mockups and went from research straight to an interactive prototype built with AI tools.

Working in code from day one meant real flows, real states and real content much earlier. Each iteration took hours instead of days, so ideas could be judged by using them rather than by looking at them.
